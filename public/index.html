      ZentEncrypted
    

    
Chat that stays yours.

    
Private rooms and friend messages are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server only ever holds scrambled data it can't read, and it runs on our own hardware, not a big tech cloud.


    

      

        
        
End-to-end encrypted

        
Private rooms and friend chats are locked with keys only you and the people you talk to have.

      

      

        
        
Rooms that burn

        
Start a room with a join code. It deletes itself on a timer or when everyone leaves.

      

      

        
        
Friends, even offline

        
Messages to friends wait for them, still encrypted, until they're back. Pictures and files too.

      

      

        
        
No ads, no tracking

        
No email or phone number needed. Photos have their hidden location data removed before upload.
